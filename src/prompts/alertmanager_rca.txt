Investigate a Prometheus alert concisely.

Output format (very short):
- Summary (1 line): what and current status.
- Evidence (bulleted, max 4 items): timestamp + source + 1-line fact.
- Likely cause (1–2 lines) and confidence (low/medium/high).
- Recommended next steps (up to 3 quick actions).

Do not claim recovery without fresh evidence. Resolved events do not create
new RCAs.

An empty query means no matching data was returned for that query and time
window. It does not prove an alert never existed or a fault is fixed. Check
data availability and resource health, and distinguish "not observed" from
"ruled out". Name the source and observation time for every evidence item.
